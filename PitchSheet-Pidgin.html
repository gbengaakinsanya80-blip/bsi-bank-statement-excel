Bank Statement Intelligence (BSI)

Wetin one clerk dey do for one hour, dis app dey do with drag-and-drop.

E dey read bank statement PDF (both normal text and printed/scanned), turn am to clean Excel, check all the money well-well.

How you go open am (2 minutes)

"Dis app dey read bank statement and turn am to clean Excel for few seconds. Normally na 30–60 minutes of typing by hand. Here — drag am, drop am, done." "E dey work for text AND scanned statement. E dey know 17 Nigerian bank layouts by itself. And e dey check every number."

Stop there. Make dem talk first before you continue.

Live demo — 5 steps

  • 1. Show the empty page. "Na so easy."
  • 2. Drop the statement. "E go know say na Access Bank by itself."
  • 3. Show the table. "42 transactions, every balance checked — math dey correct."
  • 4. Scroll + categories. "E even dey tag wetin every money dey for."
  • 5. Export → Excel. "Here na your file wey don ready."

Finish with the truth story: "If e no fit read one line well, e go tell you — e no dey hide am."

Wetin you MUST do

  • Try the demo before you meet dem. Keep screenshot as backup.
  • Small pause when table show — make dem see am before you talk.
  • Use the bank dem dey use (Kuda, GTBank, etc.).
  • Mention the limit before dem reach am (big scanned file dey slow).

Wetin you MUST NOT do

  • No overload dem with features — one hook, five steps.
  • No big English: no "RapidOCR", no "hybrid extraction".
  • No say 100% — say "anything wey e no sure, e go tell you."
  • No stand there quiet when e dey work — keep dey talk.
