# Bank Statement Intelligence (BSI) — One-Page Pitch

**AI bank statement → Excel converter · text + scanned PDFs · 17 Nigerian bank layouts · balance-checked**

> "What takes a clerk an hour now takes drag-and-drop."

---

## The 2-minute opening

> "This reads a bank statement PDF and turns it into a clean, validated Excel file in seconds. Normally that's 30–60 minutes of manual typing. Here it's drag, drop, done."

> "It handles text AND scanned statements, recognizes 17 Nigerian bank layouts automatically, and double-checks every number."

**Stop here. Let them react before continuing to the demo.**

---

## Live demo — 5 moves

1. **Show the empty dashboard.** "This is all it takes."
2. **Drop in a statement.** "It'll figure out this is Access Bank on its own."
3. **Reveal the table.** "42 transactions, every balance checked — the math adds up."
4. **Scroll + categories.** "It even tags what each payment was for."
5. **Export → Excel.** "Here's the finished file, ready for your loan officer."

Close with the honesty story: *"If a line can't be read perfectly, it flags it — it never quietly skips it."*

| Accountant / auditor | Clean exports, reconciliation | "Your Excel output is validation-ready — no re-typing." |
| Student / personal | Free tier, simplicity | "A bank clerk in your pocket." |
| Bank / large company | Security, self-hosting | "It can run on your own servers — statements never leave your network." |

---

## Do

- Run the demo before presenting; keep a screenshot as backup.
- Pause when the table appears — let them see it before you speak.
- Demo the bank *they* use (Kuda, GTBank, etc.).
- Mention limits before they hit them (large OCR scans are slow).

## Don't

- Don't overload with features — one hook, five steps.
- No jargon: no "RapidOCR", "hybrid extraction", "layout detection".
- Never claim 100% — say "anything uncertain is flagged."
- Don't stand silent while it processes — narrate.
