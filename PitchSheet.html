Do

  • Run the demo before presenting; keep a screenshot as backup.
  • Pause when the table appears — let them see it before you speak.
  • Demo the bank they use (Kuda, GTBank, etc.).
  • Mention limits before they hit them (large OCR scans are slow).

Don't

  • Don't overload with features — one hook, five steps.
  • No jargon: no "RapidOCR", "hybrid extraction", "layout detection".
  • Never claim 100% — say "anything uncertain is flagged."
  • Don't stand silent while it processes — narrate.
