🪄 My Magic Money Helper

Follow the steps — it's easy as 1, 2, 3! 🎩✨

1
Open the magic door 🚪

Open the internet on your computer and go to the helper's house (the website). Say hello! 👋

2
Give it the money paper 📬

There's a big box that says "drop your statement here". Pick up your money paper (the PDF) and drop it in — just like putting a letter in a mailbox!

3
Wait, the helper is working 🛁

Watch the progress bar fill up like a bathtub filling with water. The helper is writing down every single coin. Don't rush it!

4
Look at the treasure list 🏆

Now you see all the money: 💰 money in · 💸 money out · 🏷️ stickers called categories (like "Salary", "Transfer", "Bills"). Every coin knows what it was for!

5
Take your gold map 🗺️

Press Download and choose Excel. A new computer file appears — a shiny gold map you can open, show to mommy and daddy, or print!

🌟 The Golden Rules of the Helper 🌟

⭐ If the helper can't read a word, it tells you — it never hides it. That's a good helper!

⭐ It checks its math: money at start + money in − money out = money at the end. If it doesn't match, it says "check me!" 🔍

🎩 It knows lots of bank papers — show it a real one and watch it say "This looks like Access Bank!"
