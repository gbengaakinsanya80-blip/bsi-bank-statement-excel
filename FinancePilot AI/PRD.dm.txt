Yes. And I would not build this as merely a “PDF-to-Excel converter.” That market is already becoming crowded. Current products are moving toward AI categorisation, reconciliation and financial reporting, while newer accounting-AI research is showing that the difficult part is not extracting numbers—it is maintaining a traceable, internally consistent accounting ledger. 

Your opportunity is to build an AI Financial Statement Factory:

Bank Statement → Clean Transactions → AI Accounting Classification → Human Review → Double-Entry Journals → Ledgers → Trial Balance → Adjustments → Management Accounts → Financial Analysis

The key differentiator should be “AI proposes, accounting engine proves.” That prevents an LLM from simply inventing plausible financial statements. Recent research specifically found substantial problems when AI-generated accounting outputs are not tied back to source documents and ledger calculations.

- Total Debits

Previous Running Balance
+ Credit
- Debit
